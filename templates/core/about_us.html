Welcome to E-commerce

E-commerce is an online marketplace based in Phnom Penh. We bring together local vendors — market traders, family farms and neighbourhood shops — on one site, so you can order fresh produce and everyday groceries from all of them in a single basket.

Order before noon and we deliver the same day across Phnom Penh; elsewhere in Cambodia our courier partners usually reach you within two days. Every vendor on the site is verified before their first listing goes live, and you can see their rating and response time on their store page.

Our performance

Your Partner for e-commerce grocery solution

We handle the parts that are hard for a small vendor to do alone: the storefront, the search, the payments and the delivery. The vendor keeps doing what they are good at — sourcing and selling.

That means a shop can be online in an afternoon, with no website to build and no payment gateway to set up.

Who we are

A small team based in Phnom Penh, working with vendors across the city's markets and with farms in the surrounding provinces.

Our history

The site began as a coursework project at the Royal University of Phnom Penh and grew into a working marketplace for local vendors.

Our mission

To give small Cambodian sellers a fair route online, and to make ordering good food as simple as it should be.
