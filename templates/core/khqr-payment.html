How to pay
  1. Open your banking app and scan the QR above.
  2. Type the amount ${{ order.price }} yourself — this QR does not fill it in for you.
  3. Complete the transfer, then copy the transaction ID from your receipt.
  4. Paste it into the form and submit.

The same QR is used for every order, so your payment cannot be matched automatically. Your order is confirmed once we check the transaction ID against our bank statement.
