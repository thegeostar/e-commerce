Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 August 2026 6 mins read

Who we are

We are E-commerce, an online marketplace operated from No. 12, Street 271, Sangkat Toul Tum Poung 1, Khan Chamkarmon, Phnom Penh 120306, Cambodia. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

We handle personal information in line with the Law on Consumer Protection (2019) and the Law on Electronic Commerce (2019) of the Kingdom of Cambodia, and with Article 40 of the Constitution, which protects the privacy of residence and correspondence.

What we collect

  1. Account information — your name, email address, phone number and password when you register. Your password is stored only as a cryptographic hash; nobody at E-commerce can read it.
  2. Order information — your delivery address, the contents of your order, and the date and amount of each purchase.
  3. Payment information — handled by our payment providers. We receive a confirmation that a payment succeeded and a reference number. We never see or store your full card number.
  4. Content you post — product reviews, ratings and any images you upload.
  5. Technical information — your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, collected automatically in our server logs.

Why we use it

  1. To take payment for your order and deliver it to you.
  2. To let you sign in, see your order history and manage your saved addresses.
  3. To answer your questions and handle returns, complaints and refunds.
  4. To show your reviews to other shoppers, under the display name on your account.
  5. To keep the site secure, detect fraud, and fix faults.
  6. To send you service messages about orders you have placed. We only send marketing email if you have asked for it, and every such email has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where it is needed to run the service:

  1. Vendors — when you buy from a vendor on the marketplace, we pass them the name, delivery address and phone number needed to fulfil your order, and nothing more.
  2. Delivery partners — the courier carrying your parcel receives your name, address and phone number.
  3. Payment providers — who process the transaction. They are independent controllers of the data you give them and have their own privacy policies.
  4. Authorities — where we are required to disclose information by Cambodian law, a court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority.

Some of our providers process data on servers outside Cambodia. Where that happens we take reasonable steps to ensure the information stays protected to the standard described in this policy.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember what is in your cart, and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. These are necessary for the site to work. We also use a small number of analytics cookies to understand which pages are used; you can block cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the site — the cart and checkout in particular — will stop working correctly if you do.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open. We keep order and transaction records for 10 years after the order, as required for tax and accounting purposes in Cambodia. Server logs are kept for 90 days. When you close your account we delete or anonymise your personal information except where we are required to retain it.

How we protect it

Data is held on access-controlled servers. Passwords are hashed, connections to the site are encrypted in transit, and only staff who need access to personal information in order to do their job have it. No online service can promise perfect security, but we review our controls regularly and will notify you and the relevant authority without undue delay if a breach is likely to affect you.

Children

The Service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  1. You can see and correct your name, email, phone number and addresses at any time from your account dashboard.
  2. You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  3. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information attached to it, subject to the retention periods above.
  4. You can withdraw consent to marketing email at any time, either from the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

Write to us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and for material changes we will tell you before the change takes effect.
