Purchase Guide

Last updated 9 August 2026 4 mins read

1. Account Registering

You can browse without an account, but you need one to check out so we know where to deliver. Registration takes a minute and asks only for:

  • Full name (required)
  • Email address (required) — this is what you sign in with
  • Mobile phone number (required) — the courier calls this number
  • Password (required)
  • Delivery address (added at your first checkout)

2. Select Product

Search by name, or browse a category from the menu. On a product page choose the quantity and any options the vendor offers, then press Add to Cart. You can keep adding from different vendors — they all go into the same basket and you pay once.

3. Confirm Order Content

Open your cart to check quantities and remove anything you changed your mind about. Enter a coupon code here if you have one. At checkout, confirm the delivery address and phone number, and you will see the item total, the delivery charge and the final amount before you pay.

4.Transaction Completed

Once payment goes through you will see a confirmation page with your order ID, and the order appears under Orders in your dashboard. Orders placed before noon are delivered the same day in Phnom Penh; elsewhere in Cambodia allow up to two days.

5. Accepted Credit Cards

  • Visa
  • Mastercards
  • American Express
  • Discover
*Taxes are calculated by your local bank and location.
